# Privacy Policy — USSD-UI

_Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Publisher: iWEKOBONGstudios · Contact: essiengodspower447@gmail.com_

**Host this page at a public URL (GitHub Pages works) and paste the link in Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.**

## Summary
USSD-UI does not collect, store, transmit or share your personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that send data.

## What the app accesses and why
- **Accessibility service (only if you agree and switch it on).** While you have just dialled a USSD code from this app, the service reads the text of your phone's own USSD pop-up (for example the "Carrier info" box), whether you dialled from this app or from the regular Phone app (the latter can be switched off in Settings), so the menu can be shown as buttons, and it types your choice into that pop-up and presses its buttons for you. It does not read any other app or screen. The text is used on your screen and then discarded; it is not saved or sent anywhere. Anything you type, including PINs, is not stored.
- **Phone permission (CALL_PHONE).** Used only to start the USSD code you chose, on the SIM you chose. The app does not place voice calls.
- **Phone state permission (READ_PHONE_STATE).** Used only to list your SIM cards (slot and carrier name) so you can choose which one to dial with. This is not sent anywhere.

## Data stored on your device
Settings (such as chosen SIM, mode, and the quick codes you save) are stored locally on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Not affiliated
USSD-UI is an independent app. Network and bank names shown on quick-code buttons are only labels for publicly published USSD codes; the app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any network or bank.
