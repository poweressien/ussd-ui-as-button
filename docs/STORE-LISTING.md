## Full description
USSD-UI turns your carrier's USSD menus into real, tappable buttons.

Instead of typing "1", then "2", then "3" into a plain pop-up, dial a code from USSD-UI and every option appears as a button. Tap to choose. Amounts, account numbers and PINs get a proper keypad.

• Real dial pad with quick-access codes for banks, airtime and data
• Dial from the regular Phone app and USSD-UI turns the pop-up into buttons (optional)
• Choose which SIM to dial with on dual-SIM phones
• Save your own favourite codes
• Menus, prompts, confirmations and final messages shown clearly
• Works through Android's Accessibility service, which you switch on yourself

PRIVACY FIRST
USSD-UI has no internet permission, no ads, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing leaves your phone.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE
To show menus as buttons, USSD-UI uses Android's Accessibility service. It reads only the text of the phone's own USSD pop-up that appears after you dial a code (from the app or, if you leave that option on, from the regular Phone app), and taps that pop-up's buttons / types your choice for you. It reads nothing else. You are asked to agree before it is ever requested, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

NOT AFFILIATED
USSD-UI is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any mobile network or bank. Quick-code labels are for publicly published USSD codes. Network charges for USSD sessions follow your carrier's rates.
