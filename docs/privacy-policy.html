Privacy Policy – USSD-UI

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Publisher: iWEKOBONGstudios · Contact: essiengodspower447@gmail.com

Summary

USSD-UI does not collect, store, transmit or share your personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that send data.

What the app accesses and why

  • Accessibility service (only if you agree and switch it on). After you dial a USSD code (from this app, or from the regular Phone app if you leave that option on in Settings), the service reads the text of your phone's own USSD pop-up (for example the "Carrier info" box) so the menu can be shown as buttons. It then types your choice into that pop-up and presses its buttons for you. It does not read any other app or screen. The text is only used on your screen and then discarded. It is not saved or sent anywhere. Anything you type, including PINs, is not stored.
  • Phone permission (CALL_PHONE). Used only to start the USSD code you chose, on the SIM you chose. The app does not place voice calls.
  • Phone state permission (READ_PHONE_STATE). Used only to list your SIM cards (slot and carrier name) so you can pick which one to dial with. This is not sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Settings (such as chosen SIM, mode, and the quick codes you save) are stored locally on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. The app cannot connect to the internet.

Your control

You can switch the accessibility service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility, and turn off Phone-app capture inside the app's Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Not affiliated

USSD-UI is an independent app. Network and bank names on quick-code buttons are only labels for publicly published USSD codes. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any network or bank.
